## How to add new articles

1. Create a new `.mdx` file in `src/content/blog/en/` (and `src/content/blog/fr/` for French)
2. Use the slug as the filename (e.g. `what-is-team-celebration-software.mdx`)
3. Add the required frontmatter (see `docs/seo-content-plan-website.md` Section 6)
4. Write your content in MDX (Markdown + JSX)

## Example formatting

- **Bold** and *italic* text
- [Internal links](/features) to your site
- Lists like this one

The blog will automatically pick up new articles and display them on the index page.
